Akhada

A 2.5D power fighter across India, from the Varanasi ghats to a Himalayan monastery. Street fighters, wanderers and legends of the epics, each with five specials and two Hell moves. Fight a very hard CPU, a friend on the same keyboard, or practise on a training dummy.

How to fight
  • Chakra: the blue bar (500). Every special costs a share of it. It barely refills by itself: hold J, K and L together (Player 2: 1, 2, 3) to charge it, which leaves you open, or earn it by landing and taking hits.
  • Specials: tap Defend, then a direction, then Attack or Jump.
  • Hell moves: Defend, Forward, Jump, Attack once the bar turns gold (400). The Awakened Hell move is Defend, Up, Jump, Attack, below 30% HP: it empties the whole bar, and when it lands it takes 40% of the foe's health (20% if they block it all).
  • Super combos: land a special, then go straight into a Hell move. During a Hell move, tap Attack when your fighter flashes for extra hits.
  • Escape: tap Defend while you are being hit to break free (100 chakra). With your back to the wall it takes you over your attacker. Nobody can hit you while you are down or getting up.
  • Basics: Attack three times for a combo. Double-tap a direction to run, then Attack or Jump. Hold Defend to block. Move up and down to step out of a shot's path.
  • On a touch screen: the same sequences as the keyboard: tap Defend, push the stick, then tap Attack or Jump. The sequence you are entering shows above your fighter. Hold Charge to fill chakra.
  • Online (beta): one of you creates a room and sends the code; the other joins with it. A fast, steady connection works best.
  • Select: WASD or arrows to move, J or Enter to pick (Player 2: arrows and 1)
  • Player 1: WASD move, J attack, K jump, L defend, hold JKL to charge
  • Player 2: arrows, 1 attack, 2 jump, 3 defend, hold 123 to charge (or , . /)
  • P pause

Rollback Street

A side-scrolling brawler in the style of the late-80s arcade classics. Fight through the street, the subway, the rooftops and the foundry, alone or with a friend on the same keyboard.

Rollback Street

Four stages, four bosses. Clear a stage to unlock it here next time.

Move list
  • Combos: punch, punch, punch (uppercut). Punch, punch, kick (spin heel kick). Kick, kick (roundhouse).
  • Back elbow: tap away from the way you face, then punch, to hit someone behind you.
  • Dash: double-tap a direction. Then punch for a shoulder tackle, kick for a flying kick, jump for a long jump.
  • Grab: walk into a dazed enemy. Punch to knee, kick to throw them into the others.
  • Specials use the SP bar, which fills as you land hits: rolling kick (special, or punch and kick together, 30 SP), cyclone kick (special in the air, 25 SP), rising dragon (down, forward, punch, or down and special, 40 SP).
  • Pickups: pipes swing (every hit knocks down), knives throw, apples heal, cans refill SP. Jump over thrown knives and shockwaves.
  • Player 1: WASD move, J punch, K kick, L jump, I special
  • Player 2: arrows, 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 jump, 4 special (or , . / ')
  • P pause

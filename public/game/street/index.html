Rollback Street

Nani has vanished from her dhaba on Chandni Lane, and the only clue is a card signed R. Four stages, four bosses, one long night. Beat Raghav or Cheel and they change sides.

Move list
  • Combos: punch, punch, punch (uppercut). Punch, punch, kick (spin heel kick). Kick, kick (roundhouse).
  • Back elbow: tap away from the way you face, then punch, to hit someone behind you.
  • Run: double-tap a direction or hold Shift; on a phone, push the stick all the way to the edge. Then punch for a shoulder tackle, kick for a flying kick, jump for a long jump.
  • Grab: walk into a dazed enemy. Punch to knee, kick to throw them into the others.
  • Specials use the SP bar, which fills as you land hits: rolling kick (special, or punch and kick together, 30 SP), cyclone kick (special in the air, 25 SP), rising dragon (down, forward, punch, or down and special, 40 SP).
  • Story: four of Nani's letters are hidden in barrels, one per stage. Find them all for the true ending.
  • Fighters: each has their own Special. Rai rolls, Kai kicks like lightning. Beat Raghav or Cheel and they change sides: play as them from the next stage on, with their own power but less health and a single life.
  • Pickups: lathis swing (every hit knocks down), knives throw, samosas heal, kulhad chai refills SP. Jump over thrown knives and shockwaves.
  • Player 1: WASD move, J punch, K kick, L jump, I special, Shift dash
  • Player 2: arrows, 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 jump, 4 special (or , . / ')
  • P pause

Controls

  • Player 1: WASD move, J punch, K kick, L jump, I special, E interact, R rewind (once you have Nani's watch), Shift run
  • Player 2: arrows, 1 punch, 2 kick, 3 jump, 4 special (or , . / '), Enter interact
  • M map, Tab phone (from Act 2), Q radio station (on a scooter), U block (hold; just before a hit to parry), double-tap up or down to roll, P or Esc pause, F full screen
  • Gamepads (one or two): stick or d-pad move, X punch, A kick, B jump, Y special, LB interact, RT rewind, click the stick to run, Back map, Start pause. In menus, A chooses and B goes back.

On a touch screen the stick moves (push it to the edge to run), the four buttons fight, and a Use button appears when there is something to use.

Move list
  • Combos: punch, punch, punch (uppercut). Punch, punch, kick (spin heel kick). Kick, kick (roundhouse).
  • Back elbow: tap away from the way you face, then punch, to hit someone behind you.
  • Run: double-tap a direction or hold Shift. Then punch for a shoulder tackle, kick for a flying kick, jump for a long jump.
  • Grab: walk into a dazed enemy. Punch to knee, kick to throw them into the others.
  • Specials use the SP bar, which fills as you land hits: your own Special (or punch and kick together), cyclone kick (special in the air, 25 SP), rising dragon (down, forward, punch, 40 SP).
  • Pickups: lathis swing, knives throw, samosas heal, kulhad chai refills SP. Jump over thrown knives and shockwaves.
